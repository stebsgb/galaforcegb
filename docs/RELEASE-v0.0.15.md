Build: `galaforce-0.0.15.gb` (MBC1 + RAM + battery, 32 KB ROM).

## New
- **Boss explosions.** Every bullet hit on the boss shows a full explosion with its sound. During the last 5 hits, explosions keep appearing one at a time over the boss, and the boss's death lasts longer.

## Changes
- **Smart bomb fix.** A lost life no longer resets your smart bomb. Bombs you hold stay with you, and a new bomb is added only when the boss is killed (up to 2 held).
